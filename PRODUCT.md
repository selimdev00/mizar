# PRODUCT.md - mizar (friend Lee landing rebrand concept)

register: brand

## What this is

A from-scratch landing concept for **friend Lee**, a web-production studio ("дружелюбный production, который хочется обнять"). This is a rebrand/redesign concept built from the studio's own deck and brand, used as a portfolio piece. The design IS the product: a marketing landing whose job is to make the studio feel sharp, friendly and current in the first three seconds.

## Users

- Prospective clients evaluating a web studio: direct clients, agencies, and large integrators.
- They skim. They judge craft instantly. They have seen a hundred generic studio sites.
- Secondary: hiring managers at the studio reviewing this as an application artifact.

## Brand

- Personality: friendly but technical. Playful confidence, not corporate. "Production you want to hug."
- Signature marks: the `>_` terminal prompt in the wordmark, and the round acid-lime **happy-face** smiley (their real brand asset).
- Voice: short, direct, warm, Russian. Lowercase-leaning in casual spots, bold and loud in headlines.
- Texture: high-contrast, near-black canvas, one electric acid-lime, generous bold wide-grotesk type, full-bleed rhythm. Confident negative space.

## Tone

Bold, warm, a little irreverent. Never stocky, never enterprise-blue, never "we are passionate about digital solutions."

## Anti-references

- Generic agency templates: centered 1200px container, three identical icon cards, stocky hero photo.
- Enterprise SaaS blue. Gradient-text headlines. Glassmorphism. Hero-metric template.
- Cluttered floating decorative doodads competing with the content (explicitly removed).

## Strategic principles

1. The smiley and the acid lime carry the identity. Everything else is near-black and type.
2. Full-bleed, consistent gutter. No narrow centered container, no per-section drift.
3. Proof over claim: real case screenshots and links, not adjective lists.
4. Motion is calm and purposeful (marquee, reveals, breathing smiley). No bounce, no confetti.
5. If it reads as "AI made a studio site", it failed.
